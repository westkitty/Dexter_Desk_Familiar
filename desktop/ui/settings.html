Sound & speech

Speech input (microphone)

Optional and off. When on, a microphone button appears in the conversation panel. Dexter records only between your two clicks (up to 30 seconds), transcribes on this computer with a whisper.cpp program you installed, deletes the audio, and puts the text in the box for you to review. He never listens otherwise. macOS will ask for microphone permission the first time.

Conversation

Local AI

Optional. When off, Dexter makes no network requests at all. When on, only this app contacts the endpoint below, only when you send a message or press Test. Nothing is downloaded and no model is installed for you.

Notes & memory

Notes are things you ask Dexter to remember (also with /remember). They are stored only on this computer. A firewall keeps them out of AI requests unless you allow it; every crossing is counted in the Inspector.

      Accessibility

      Every Dexter action is available from the menu-bar icon and its keyboard-navigable menu. The conversation panel and this window are fully keyboard operable; Escape closes the conversation.

      Privacy

      • No analytics, accounts, telemetry or crash uploads.
      • No keyboard, screen, clipboard, browser or app monitoring. Dexter sees only clicks on himself.
      • No microphone or camera. Permission requests are refused automatically.
      • Network: none, unless Local AI is enabled — then only to your endpoint.
      • Stored locally: these settings, and the conversation only if you enable it.

      Reset & recovery
      
      
      If Dexter is invisible or ignores clicks: use the menu-bar icon → Restore Dexter, or open the app again from Applications. Click-through mode always turns off when Dexter restarts.
